A Quick "Chris-Style" Strategic Tip:
By making them click this link, you aren't just being GDPR-compliant—you are training your lead. You are showing them that your insights are valuable and protected. A "confirmed" email address in your database is worth 10x more than a "raw" one because you know there is a real person behind it who is willing to take action.
A Quick Technical Note on Security:
Once the user clicks the link in the email, your FastAPI backend should:
Validate the token.
Mark the lead as is_verified = True in your database.
Return the pre-calculated ML results in the JSON response so the React page can render the chart.
