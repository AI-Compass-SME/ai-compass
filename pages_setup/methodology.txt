2. Why this builds trust (The "Technical" Logic)
Dimension Normalization: Mentions that you scale data (0 to 1). This shows you understand that raw scores shouldn't distort the model.
Archetypes over "Scores": Using the term "Cluster" and "Centroid" sounds far more professional than just saying "the AI calculated it."
k-NN vs. K-Means: Explaining that you use two different methods (one for grouping, one for comparison) suggests a sophisticated pipeline.
The "Stack" Footer: Listing the technologies (Scikit-Learn, AES-256) reassures CTOs that you are using industry-standard tools.
3. How to use this for Go-Live
Create a route at /methodology.
Add a small link in your Result Page's footer: "Curious about the math? View our Methodology Fact Sheet."
PDF Export: If you generate a PDF for your leads, include a simplified version of this as an "Appendix" at the end of the report. It adds massive credibility to your consulting offer.
