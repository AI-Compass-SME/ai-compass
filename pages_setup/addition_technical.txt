1. Professional Data Security Clause
Add this section to your Privacy Policy (Datenschutzerklärung). It uses the specific legal and technical terminology that signals "High Maturity."
Data Security and Technical Measures
To protect your data against accidental or intentional manipulation, loss, or unauthorized access by third parties, we use state-of-the-art technical and organizational measures (TOMs):
Encryption in Transit: All communication between your browser and our servers is protected by industry-standard TLS 1.2/1.3 encryption (HTTPS).
Encryption at Rest: All personal data and assessment results stored in our database are encrypted at rest using AES-256. This ensures that even in the event of physical server access, your data remains unreadable.
Data Minimization: We only collect the specific data points required for the K-Means clustering algorithm. Your IP address is anonymized in our analysis logs.
EU-Based Hosting: Your data is processed exclusively on ISO 27001-certified servers located within the European Union ([Name of Provider, e.g., Hetzner/AWS Frankfurt]).
2. Technical Go-Live Checklist (Postgres & FastAPI)
To make the above statement true, you should ensure your backend is "hardened":
Postgres Encryption: If you use a managed DB (like AWS RDS or Supabase), ensure "Storage Encryption" is toggled ON. If you are self-hosting on a VPS, use LUKS for disk encryption or at least ensure your DB connection requires SSL (sslmode=require).
Pydantic Validation: Use Pydantic's EmailStr and Field(max_length=...) in your FastAPI schemas to prevent malicious payloads or database overflows.
Production Logging: Disable all print() statements and debug=True flags. Use a structured logger that filters out the email field so it never appears in your server logs.
3. Boosting Trust with "Verification Levels"
If you want to appear even more trustworthy, consider these two subtle UI elements:
The "Last Updated" Badge: Place a small note in the footer or near the results: "Benchmarks last updated: [Current Month] 2026." This shows the tool is active and maintained.
The "Human-in-the-loop" Option: On the results page, offer a "Report Correction" button. "Does this result feel inaccurate? Click here to have our strategy team manually review your data." This turns a potential error into a high-value consulting lead.
4. A Note on "Google Fonts" (Final Warning)
Since this is your actual Go-Live: Check your index.html one last time. If you see <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com..." />, you are at risk for an "Abmahnung" (cease and desist) in Germany.
Action: Download the .woff2 files, put them in /public/fonts, and load them via CSS @font-face.
