Legal Basis: The processing is based on your consent (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. a GDPR) or for the performance of a contract-like relationship (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b GDPR).
Automated Data Processing (Machine Learning)
Our system uses automated algorithms to process your inputs:
Clustering: We use a K-Means Clustering algorithm to assign your company to one of five maturity levels.
Benchmarking: We use a k-Nearest Neighbors (k-NN) algorithm to compare your data against a global dataset to identify strategic gaps.
