Consent Checkbox (Double Opt-In for leads)
To ensure your AI Compass is GDPR-compliant and creates high-quality leads, you should implement this lead-capture form as a "gateway" between the last assessment question and the results page.
In Germany and the EU, Double Opt-In (DOI) is the gold standard. This means:
The user fills out the form and ticks the box.
You send an automated "Confirmation" email via your provider (e.g., Brevo).
The user clicks a link in that email. Only then is their lead "verified" for your consulting follow-up.

The Double Opt-In Workflow  To make this fully legal and professional, your FastAPI backend should handle the logic like this:
Submission: When the user clicks "Generate," send the data to your /api/v1/leads endpoint.
Verification Email: The backend generates a unique verification_token and sends an email via Brevo saying: "Please confirm your email to view your AI Compass Roadmap."
The Reveal: You have two options here:
The "Teaser" approach: Show the Radar Chart immediately, but keep the "Strategic Roadmap" (the high-value part) locked behind the email confirmation link.
The "Professional" approach: Show a "Success!" message: "Check your inbox. We've sent a confirmation link. Your custom roadmap is ready and waiting."
Why this works for you (The Strategy)  Filter Quality: By asking for "Number of Employees" and "Industry," your ML model can actually refine its peer benchmarking. You can say: "Compared to other companies in [Industry] with [Size] employees, you are a Leader."
Consultancy Hook: The "Work Email" requirement ensures you don't just get spiderman123@gmail.com addresses, but actual business leads.
Legal Safety: The "I can withdraw my consent at any time" sentence in the checkbox is a mandatory GDPR requirement.
Visualizing the Interaction  The Screen: The radar chart is blurred in the background. In the foreground, a clean, white card asks for the details. The Effect: It creates a "Value Exchange." The user has already spent 2-3 minutes answering questions; they are highly likely to provide their email to see the result of their effort.
