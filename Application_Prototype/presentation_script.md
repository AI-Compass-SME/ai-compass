# MVP Frontend Presentation Script (3 Minutes)

**Goal:** Demonstrate the user journey from landing page to insights.

## 1. Landing Page (0:00 - 0:30)
* Moving to the frontend MVP, we've designed this to be actionable immediately. We skip the marketing fluff and get users straight to the assessment.

## 2. Company Information (0:30 - 0:45)
* We keep the onboarding frictionless. Just a few key details to personalize the benchmarking engine.

## 3. Assessment - One Dimension (0:45 - 1:30)
* The assessment is structured by the 7 key dimensions you see on the left sidebar, each containing a specific set of questions. For this demo, I'll only walk you through the Strategy & Business Vision dimension.

* Also, you'll notice we use different question types throughout the assessment to ensure we capture the necessary depth without overcomplicating the user experience.

* While I answer these, it's important to note that our engine doesn't just calculate a simple average.

* Each question and answer option carries a specific strategic weight. This allows us to differentiate between foundational requirements and advanced differentiators.

* These weighted inputs are exactly what feed into the sophisticated scoring model we'll see on the results page.

* Now, while the full questionnaire flow is live and fully functional, for the sake of this demo's timing, we'll fast-forward to the results of a completed assessment.

## 4. Results & Model Explanation (1:30 - 3:00)
* Here is the comprehensive report generated by our intelligence engine. It goes beyond simple scoring to provide context.

### A. Maturity Score
* First, the overall Maturity Score and Industry Percentile. This gives the company an immediate high-level baseline of where they stand today against their peers.

### B. Benchmarking (Cluster Profile)
* This is our **Hybrid Clustering** engine in action. Instead of static tiers, we use **K-Means clustering** to dynamically assign companies to peer archetypes. It tells the user: 'You aren't just a score; you belong to this specific behavioral group relative to the market.'

### C. Dimension Layout (Maturity Profile)
* Here we break down that score into specific dimensions, displayed as a radar chart. This visualization highlights disproportionate strengths or weaknesses that might be hidden by the average score.

### D. Strategic Gap Analysis
* This is the most critical section: **Strategic Gap Analysis**. We use **Statistical Anomaly Detection** to find structural imbalances. Here, for example, we see a structural imbalance between **Data Readiness & Literacy** and **Use Cases & Business Value**, alongside a critical gap in **Clarity**. This indicates that while the company wants to deploy advanced AI, their data foundation isn't ready to support it.

### E. Next Best Action (Roadmap)
* Based on those gaps, our model generates a 3-Phase Transformation Roadmap. It prioritizes **Foundation** steps to **Fix Data Readiness & Literacy Gaps** and ensure **Clarity** in Phase 1, before moving to **AI Tooling Stack** in Phase 2, and finally **Execution Maturity** in Phase 3.

### F. Expert Consultation & Next Steps
* If the automated roadmap isn't enough, we offer a direct bridge to human expertise. Users can get in touch to dive deeper into these specific findings.

### G. Download Report & Closing
* Finally, users can download the full detailed report. This allows them to share these insights internally with stakeholders and keep a permanent record of their baseline.

* Now, I'll pass it back to Christian to show you what's next for AI Compass.

## Technical Appendix: Architecture Verification
* **Slide Validation**: The "Dual-engine ML pipeline" slide is **technically accurate** based on the current `ml_v5` codebase.
* **Cluster Engine**: Verified usage of **K-Means** with `n_clusters=5` (Traditionalist to Leader).
* **Gap Analyzer**: Verified usage of **Z-Score** (StandardScaler) for anomaly detection and weighted rule-based logic for critical gaps.
* **Roadmap Generator**: Verified usage of **k-NN** (`NearestNeighbors`, `n_neighbors=15`, `metric='cosine'`) to identify "Peer Neighbors".
* **Stack**: Verified **FastAPI** backend and **React/Vite** frontend.
